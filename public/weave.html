WEAVE

Charged nodes rain into a pile. Drag one stroke through touching nodes of the same charge to wire a circuit — diagonals count.

Release to discharge: a current rips down the wire you drew and every node on it detonates. A longer snake pays far more. But a node left too long oxidises into dead rust that breaks your paths — it only crumbles when you discharge a wire beside it. Keep the circuit turning over, or the pile buries you to the ceiling.
