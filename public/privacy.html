Privacy Policy

Anorak Arcade · last updated 14 June 2026

Anorak Arcade is a collection of small games with global leaderboards. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run those leaderboards. We do not sell your data, show third‑party ads, or use third‑party tracking or analytics SDKs.

What we collect

  • A device identifier — a random ID generated on your device to attribute your scores and playtime. It is not linked to your real identity unless you sign in.
  • Gameplay data — the games you play, your scores, total playtime, and number of plays, so we can show leaderboards and your stats.
  • A display name & avatar — the handle and emoji you choose to appear on the leaderboards. Optional.
  • Sign in with Apple (optional) — if you choose to sign in, we receive a stable Apple user identifier and, if you allow it, your email (which may be Apple's private relay). This is used only to link your scores to your account across devices. You can use the entire app without signing in.

How we use it

  • To run global, friends, daily and seasonal leaderboards and show your rank and stats.
  • To let you follow other players and see activity.
  • To operate and improve the games.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your data.
  • We don't run third‑party advertising or tracking.
  • We don't collect your contacts, location, or device sensors.

Storage & sharing

Data is stored on Cloudflare infrastructure used to run the service. Your display name, avatar, scores and ranks are public on the leaderboards by design. We share data only as needed to operate the service or where required by law.

Your choices

  • Play anonymously — signing in is optional.
  • Reset your local stats from the app/site at any time.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing us.

Children

Anorak Arcade is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Contact
