Slide to position your ship — it auto-fires upward. Enemies assemble into a formation, then peel off in dives; a diving kill is worth double a formed one. A captor can tractor-beam your ship for a life — but shoot it down while it holds you to recover and fly as two: double fire and double score. Clear a wave without losing a ship for a perfect bonus. Lose all your ships and the sortie ends.
