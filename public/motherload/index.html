HOW TO PLAY

Four steps. Then just keep digging.

1
DIG DOWN
Hold ↓ to drill into the ground. Press ←/→ into dirt to tunnel sideways.
2
COLLECT ORE
Minerals load into your cargo automatically. The deeper you go, the richer they get.
3
FLY HOME & SELL
Thrust back ↑ to the surface base. Sell at the Mineral Depot, top up at the Fuel Station, fix up at the Repair Bay.
4
UPGRADE & REPEAT
Spend your earnings on a bigger tank, stronger drill and more cargo — then dig deeper than ever.

⚠ Keep an eye on FUEL (you need enough to climb home) and HULL (lava, gas and long falls hurt). Out of fuel? Press R for a paid rescue.

A NOTE FROM THE DEV

This is our little love letter to Motherload — the 2004 Flash classic by XGen Studios that ate so many of our afternoons.

We rebuilt it from scratch in just a few days, as a challenge: no game engine, no downloaded assets, no build step. Every tile, every sound, every star in the sky is generated in code.

It's free, and it's a demo in the truest sense — a proof of what a few days of obsession can make. If you enjoy it, we'd love nothing more than to keep digging: more biomes, more story, more depth, more pod.

All feedback is genuinely welcome. Tell us what felt great, what felt clunky, and what you'd want to see next.

— Thanks for playing. Now go strike the Motherlode. ⛏
