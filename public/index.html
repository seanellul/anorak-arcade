▶ THE PROTOTYPES

CINDER

containment · draw walls

A fire spreads across the grid. Wall it into sealed pockets to suffocate it. Bigger captures pay more — but the longer you wait, the faster it spreads.

order-from-chaos · risk/reward release

SHIFT

shifting field · slide rows

Mineral layers push up from below. Slide rows to line up big groups — but matches only resolve on the rise. Time the Shift for a cascade.

perpetual pressure · order-from-chaos

CONDUIT

rhythmic routing · rotate nodes

A source fires a pulse every beat toward a lit sink. Rotate nodes to connect a live path before the beat lands. The beat is the metronome of tension.

tension→resolution rhythm · self-scaling

HOMEOSTAT

equilibrium · feed meters

Reactors drain on their own clocks. One feed stream, one at a time. Keep every needle in its green flow band — a starve or overload ends the run.

perpetual pressure · loss-aversion
