Anorak Arcade

Four playable game loops, each engineered from the 8 design pillars behind Tetris — perpetual pressure, order from chaos, tractable decisions under uncertainty, tension→resolution rhythm, transparent state, mastery ceiling, fairness, self-scaling difficulty — and the neuro-loop underneath (Zeigarnik open-loops → dopaminergic wanting → skill action → prediction-error release). None uses falling tetrominoes or line clears. Each attacks the same psychology with a different core verb.

ENCLOSE

CINDER

containment · draw walls

A fire spreads across the grid. Wall it into sealed pockets to suffocate it. Bigger captures pay more — but the longer you wait, the faster it spreads. The risk/reward gamble is Tetris's "go for the Tetris" decision, made of fire.

leans on · order-from-chaos · risk/reward release
 SHIFT

STRATA

shifting field · slide rows

Mineral layers push up from below. Slide whole rows sideways (they wrap) to align 3+ of a color — they crystallize, clear, and cascade. You never place a piece; you rearrange the field that's burying you.

leans on · perpetual pressure · order-from-chaos
 ROTATE

CONDUIT

rhythmic routing · rotate nodes

A source fires a pulse every beat toward a lit sink. Rotate nodes to connect a path before the beat lands. Deliver to score and cool the core; miss and overload climbs. The beat is the metronome of tension and release.

leans on · tension→resolution rhythm · self-scaling

HOMEOSTAT

equilibrium · feed meters

Reactors drain on their own clocks. You have one feed stream and can only tend one at a time. Keep every needle in its green flow band; a starve or overload ends the run. Plate-spinning as pure loss-aversion.

leans on · perpetual pressure · tractable decisions
